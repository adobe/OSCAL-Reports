🚀 AWS Auto-Scaling Architecture for OSCAL + Ollama

Recommended Setup Cost

~$99.67/month

Annual: ~$1,196 | 1hr idle, t3.2xlarge 32 GB | Saves vs Always-On

Design: Config and users reside on EBS at /opt/oscal/data (no S3 mount on RHEL). ec2_automation backs up config, users, and logs to S3 every 10 min (max 10 min data loss if instances are replaced). S3 cost remains negligible.
